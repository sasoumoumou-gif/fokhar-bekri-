Heritage & Vision

Why Fokhar Bekri?

Nabeul has been the beating heart of Mediterranean pottery for millenia. However, modernization poses a challenge to preserving this oral and manual heritage.

Preserving Living Craft

Capturing the wisdom of master potters (*Maâlems*), documented clay formulations, natural glazing recipes, and ancient hand-throwing techniques.

Inspiring Youth

Rebranding traditional ceramics with vibrant, modern UI/UX design, interactive workshops, and gamified discovery to excite younger generations.

Empowering Artisans

Directly linking consumers, tourists, and students to local family ateliers in Nabeul, encouraging creative tourism and fair craftsman compensation.

Ancestral Techniques

The 4 Pillars of Nabeul Pottery

01 / Préparation

Argile & Pétrissage

Extracting rich sedimentary clay from local quarries, purified through water basins and hand-kneaded to remove air bubbles.

02 / Façonnage

Le Tournage Traditionnel

Shaping vessels on foot-powered artisan potter's wheels, giving birth to iconic Goullas, amphoras, and tagines with precise touch.

03 / Décoration

Émaillage & Peinture

Application of natural mineral glazes: copper green (*Vert Nabeul*), iron yellow (*Ocre*), and cobalt oxide blue with horsehair brushes.

04 / Cuisson

Four Traditionnel

Firing in traditional wood and oil kilns reaching over 950°C to vitrify the enamel and produce timeless acoustic ceramic resonance.
